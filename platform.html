The details that keep a test fair and calm.

Exact timer

A live countdown on every screen, and automatic submission when time is up.

Saved on every click

Safe resume

If a phone dies or a lab machine restarts, the student picks up where they left off.

Stays on the test

Leaving full screen, switching tabs or copying counts as a warning. After four, the test locks until a teacher unlocks it.

One device per student
