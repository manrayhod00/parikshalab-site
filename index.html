No, not for the free mock. Your browser remembers your attempt, so you can come back to your result. Create a free account only if you want a 10-mock series.

A full paper on the latest NTA pattern and syllabus. NEET UG: 180 questions in Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology, 720 marks. JEE Main: 75 questions (60 MCQ + 15 numerical) in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, 300 marks. Both are 3 hours with +4 / −1 marking.
