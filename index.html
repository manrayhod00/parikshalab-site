The pattern has changed

Both national entrance exams now live on a screen.

Pen and paper practice no longer matches what your students will face. ParikshaLab closes that gap from day one.

NEET UG

NEET moves to computer based testing.

The national testing agency has confirmed the shift of NEET UG to a computer based format. Screen reading, on screen navigation and palette discipline are now part of the syllabus in practice, and a student who has only ever used an OMR sheet starts the exam at a disadvantage.

Official circular reference. Link the notice PDF here so your parents and staff can read the source.
JEE Main and Advanced

JEE has always been a screen exam.

Numerical entry with an on screen keypad, section wise timers, marked for review states and a live question palette. Students who practise these controls every month walk in calm, and calm is worth marks.

Numerical keypad, section timers and review states, exactly as in the live exam interface.

Student analysis

Every attempt answers one question. What should I fix next?

A score tells a student where they stand. ParikshaLab tells them what to do on Monday morning.

  • Accuracy against attempts. Separates the careless from the unprepared, which need opposite remedies.
  • Chapter and topic strength. Ranked weak areas with the exact questions that exposed them.
  • Progress across tests. One line that shows whether the last month of effort actually moved anything.
